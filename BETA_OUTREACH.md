# Beta Outreach — Responsible, Zero-Budget

## Goal

Find a small number of qualified early users without spam, scraping, fake reviews, or paid advertising.

## Who to approach

People who publicly discuss recurring spreadsheet cleanup, CSV consolidation, vendor exports, sales operations, or repetitive reporting tasks and where the platform permits relevant product sharing.

## Message

> Hi! I built a small Windows tool for recurring Excel/CSV cleanup, standardization, batch processing, and merging. It runs locally for the core workflow. I'm looking for a few people who regularly repeat these spreadsheet tasks and would be willing to test an early version. No need to share sensitive data; a synthetic/sample file works. If it's relevant to what you're doing, I can point you to the public beta docs.

## Follow-up

Only follow up when the person has shown interest. Ask which spreadsheet task is most repetitive and whether the tool actually saves time.

## Record

Track aggregate counts only:

- qualified conversations
- trials started
- successful workflows
- repeat-use signals
- specific paid-feature requests

Do not retain personal contact information without permission.
