# Product Introduction Message

## One-sentence pitch

Excel Automation Tool helps small teams clean, standardize, inspect, batch-process, and merge spreadsheet files with a simple Windows workflow.

## Short message

> Still cleaning CSV and Excel files by hand? Excel Automation Tool automates repetitive cleanup, standardizes common fields, processes folders in batch, and generates quality reports — without requiring spreadsheet contents to be uploaded to a hosted service for the core workflow.

## Qualification questions

Ask prospects:

1. What spreadsheet task do you repeat every week?
2. How many files or rows are involved?
3. Which mistakes cost you the most time?
4. What output format do you need?
5. Would a repeatable one-click workflow be useful?

## Do not claim

- Guaranteed savings
- Guaranteed accuracy
- Guaranteed revenue
- Full compatibility with every Excel feature
- Legal/compliance certification

## Goal of outreach

The goal of early outreach is learning and finding qualified users, not mass unsolicited messaging. Contact people only where the channel permits relevant product discussion.
