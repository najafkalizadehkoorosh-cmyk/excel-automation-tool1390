# Zero-Budget Sales Plan

## Positioning

**Excel Automation Tool** helps small teams turn repetitive spreadsheet chores into repeatable local workflows.

The first commercial wedge is not "Excel for everyone". It is:

> Clean, standardize, batch-process, and merge recurring CSV/Excel exports without uploading the files to a hosted service.

## Best-fit early users

Prioritize people who:

- receive spreadsheet exports from vendors, marketplaces, CRMs, or internal teams;
- repeat similar cleanup steps every week or month;
- work with enough files that manual cleanup is annoying;
- care about keeping source data on their own computer.

Examples include operations assistants, small e-commerce teams, analysts, agencies, and founders handling recurring exports.

## Offer hypothesis

### Free

- Local desktop application
- Single-file cleanup
- Basic quality inspection
- General cleanup workflow

### Pro concept

The first paid offer should be based on validated demand, not an invented feature list. Candidate premium capabilities:

- unlimited or higher-volume batch workflows;
- customer-specific presets;
- advanced quality rules;
- richer exportable reports;
- reusable workflow configurations.

Do not charge for a capability until users confirm it is valuable and the licensing/distribution model is reviewed.

## First revenue target

Target: **$50/month**, not guaranteed.

Illustrative paths:

- 5 customers × $10/month = $50/month
- 2 customers × $25/month = $50/month

These are planning scenarios, not forecasts.

## Zero-cost customer validation

Use only permission-based, relevant conversations. Do not send bulk unsolicited messages.

1. Show the product to qualified spreadsheet-heavy users.
2. Ask what repetitive spreadsheet task they currently repeat.
3. Offer the free MVP for a real but non-sensitive test file or synthetic example.
4. Measure successful workflows.
5. Ask whether a specific premium capability would be worth paying for.
6. Record only aggregate validation results unless a person explicitly gives permission to retain contact details.

## Success metrics

For the first validation cycle:

- 10 qualified conversations
- 3 successful workflow trials
- 2 users expressing willingness to pay for a specific capability
- at least 1 repeat-use signal

The target is learning and evidence, not guaranteed revenue.

## Messaging

### One-line pitch

"A local-first Windows tool that cleans, standardizes, batch-processes, and merges recurring Excel/CSV files in a few clicks."

### Problem-first pitch

"Still cleaning the same CSVs every week? Excel Automation Tool turns repeatable cleanup, standardization, and file-merging steps into a simple local workflow."

### Trust point

"Core desktop processing is local-first; the MVP does not require uploading spreadsheet contents to a hosted service."

## Channel strategy

Start with communities and marketplaces where spreadsheet automation is already discussed. Adapt to each platform's rules and only respond where promotional/self-promotion activity is allowed.

Do not use automated scraping, unsolicited bulk messaging, fake reviews, fake urgency, or misleading claims.

## Revenue guardrails

No paid infrastructure is required for validation. Before accepting money, verify the lawful payment/distribution option for the seller and intended market, including any age, identity, tax, and platform requirements.
