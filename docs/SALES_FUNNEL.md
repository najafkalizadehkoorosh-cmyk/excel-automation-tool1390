# Sales Funnel (Zero Budget)

This project does not activate a payment provider or paid marketing service by default.

## Funnel

**Discovery** → GitHub / allowed community discussion / organic search

**Evaluation** → Landing page + synthetic demo + Quick Start

**Activation** → Successful first cleanup or batch workflow

**Validation** → User reports a repeated problem and asks for a premium capability

**Purchase** → A lawful payment/distribution route chosen after verifying seller eligibility

## Manual scorecard

Use a simple spreadsheet to record:

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Visitors | People who reach the product page |
| Downloads | People who download the app |
| Activated | People who successfully process a sample file |
| Qualified | People with a recurring spreadsheet problem |
| Paid interest | People who explicitly ask about a paid capability |
| Customers | Completed lawful purchases |

## Target

The first business target is **$50/month**, treated as a validation goal rather than a forecast.

Example math:

- 5 customers × $10/month
- 10 customers × $5/month

The actual price should be learned from customer demand and the chosen payment channel.
