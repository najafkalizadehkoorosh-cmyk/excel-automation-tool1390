# Product Positioning

## Audience

Small businesses, freelancers, operations staff, and analysts who repeatedly receive CSV/Excel exports and spend time cleaning or combining them.

## Core problem

Repeated spreadsheet cleanup is easy to understand but tedious to repeat: removing empty structure, normalizing text and columns, finding quality problems, processing many files, and combining exports.

## Product promise

Make recurring spreadsheet cleanup and file-processing jobs repeatable with a lightweight local-first Windows workflow.

## Differentiation hypothesis

The product should compete on simplicity and focused workflows rather than attempting to replace Excel or broad automation platforms.

## Validation rule

Do not add a feature merely because it sounds useful. Prefer features tied to a repeated customer problem, measurable activation, or explicit willingness to pay.
